The shop

A pantry for the street.

Al Madina is a working halal grocer in the middle of Freeman Street — a few doors down from the post office, on the stretch of road that has fed Grimsby's families for a hundred years.

We stock what the neighbourhood actually cooks: halal meat from trusted butchers, sacks of rice, fresh herbs by the bunch, the spices you measure by handful, and the small things that mean home.

Come in for the weekly shop, the late-evening top-up, or the one specific tin of ghee you can't find at the supermarket. We're independent, locally owned, and quietly proud of the shelf.
